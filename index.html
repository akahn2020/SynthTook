SYNTHTOOK · QUICK START

1 · GET STARTED

Click POWER ON. Browsers block audio until you click something, so this isn't optional. Plug in a USB MIDI keyboard before or after — the device dropdown updates live.

2 · PLAY NOTES

  • MIDI keyboard — pick it from the dropdown, then play.
  • Mouse — click the on-screen keys.
  • Computer keys — a w s e d f t g y h u j k covers one octave starting at middle C.

3 · MAKE A SEQUENCE

The grid below the controls is a piano roll. Rows = pitch (C5 top to C3 bottom), columns = 16 steps. Click any cell to place a note. Multiple cells in the same column = a chord. Set BPM, hit ▶ PLAY. The cyan column highlights the current step. You can play live on the keyboard at the same time the sequence runs. CLEAR wipes the pattern.

4 · RECORD FROM THE KEYBOARD

Click ● REC (it pulses magenta when armed). Two ways to record:

  • Step record — sequencer stopped. A dashed magenta outline marks the cursor step. Play a note (or chord, within ~80ms) on your MIDI keyboard / on-screen keys / computer keys; it lands on that step and the cursor advances. Press Space or → to skip a step (rest).
  • Live record — press PLAY first, then arm REC. Notes you play are written onto whichever step is sounding right now. Loop the pattern and overdub.

Notes are recorded only if their pitch falls in the C3–C5 piano-roll range. Click REC again to disarm. The sound your shaped synth is making at the moment of recording is what plays back — but you can tweak the controls afterward and the same notes will sound different.

5 · SHAPE THE SOUND

  • OSCILLATOR — SIN (pure tone), TRI (mellow), SQR (hollow / 8-bit), SAW (bright, buzzy synthwave default). Switch live, even mid-note.
  • AMP ENVELOPE — how loudness shapes a note. Attack = fade-in. Decay = drop from peak. Sustain = level held while the key is down. Release = fade-out after letting go.
  • FILTER — Cutoff sets the brightness ceiling (low = dark, high = bright). Resonance emphasizes the cutoff frequency.
  • FILTER ENV — sweeps the filter on every note. Amount = how many Hz it sweeps. Attack/Decay = sweep shape. The classic synth "wow".
